Handyman services in Singapore

The small jobs that pile up around an HDB flat or condo: a door that will not shut, a dripping tap, a TV still leaning against the wall, a flat-pack wardrobe in its box. RK E&C sends its own crew, quotes a fixed price for the job, and does not charge by the hour.

What a handyman job covers

Most calls fall into one of these. If a job is not listed, it is still worth asking, because the same crew does renovation, reinstatement and cleaning work, so the range is wider than a one-man handyman service.

  • Mounting and drilling. TV brackets, shelves, mirrors, cabinets and rails, anchored into the right wall for the load. See the guide on mounting a TV on an HDB wall.
  • Doors and hinges. Doors that drag, drop or will not latch, plus lock and handle changes and full door replacement. See HDB door repair and replacement cost.
  • Taps, pipes and leaks. Dripping taps, loose mixers, leaking traps and slow drains. See leaking tap or pipe in an HDB and kitchen sink choke.
  • Silicone and regrouting. Mouldy or split joints around sinks, basins, shower trays and worktops. See bathroom regrouting and silicone sealant.
  • Fixture fixing. Lights, fans, taps, sinks, toilet bowls, curtain rails and blinds, supplied by us or by you. See curtain rail and blinds installation.
  • Furniture assembly. Flat-pack and imported goods unpacked, assembled, levelled and wall-anchored. See furniture assembly price list and Taobao furniture assembly.
  • Patching and touch-up. Filling old anchor holes, skimming damaged plaster and repainting the patch. See peeling paint and wall repair.
  • Cabinets and carpentry fixing. Hanging wall units, adjusting doors and replacing hinges and runners. See cabinet installation cost.

A real job, with the numbers

A 689 sqft unit at One-North Eden needed to be handed back in condition. The whole reinstatement came to $850: move-out deep clean $500, four aircon units serviced at $25 each, and make-good and repaint at $400, less a bundling discount. One crew, one visit, one invoice. A cleaning-only firm would have covered the first line and left the make-good to someone else.

That is the reason to use a contractor rather than a handyman for anything that touches paint, tiling or waterproofing: the same team that patches the wall also repaints it, so there is no second trip and no colour mismatch.

How it works

Common questions

How much does a handyman cost in Singapore?

It depends on the job rather than an hourly rate. Small fixed jobs start around $50. We quote a fixed price per job after a free site visit or a few photos on WhatsApp, so there is no hourly meter running.

Do you do small jobs, or only full renovations?

Both. A single leaking tap is a fine reason to call. Because the same crew also does renovation and reinstatement, several small jobs can be bundled into one visit.

Which areas of Singapore do you cover?

All of Singapore, HDB flats and condominiums island-wide, with no travel surcharge by estate.

Can you supply the parts, or must I buy them?

Either. We can supply taps, hinges, brackets and curtain rails, or install what you have already bought, including Taobao and flat-pack goods.

Is the quote fixed?

Yes. After a site visit or photos you get a fixed price for the agreed scope in writing.
