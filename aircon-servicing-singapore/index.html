Which service do you need?

  • Cooling is fine, it is just due: general servicing.
  • Water dripping from the indoor unit: general servicing. It is nearly always a choked drain pipe or tray, not gas.
  • Weak cooling or a musty smell after servicing: chemical wash. Normal servicing cannot reach the dirt packed into the coil fins.
  • Not serviced in years, black dust blowing out, or the blower is coated: chemical overhaul.
  • Cold air but weak, and the pipe outside is not cold: pressure check, then gas top-up if it reads low.
  • Gas needed again within months: there is a leak. Topping up again only buys time; the joint or pipe needs repairing.

How often to service

Every three months for units that run every day, which is also what most Singapore tenancy agreements ask tenants to do. A unit used only at night can go four to six months. Bedrooms with the aircon on all night choke faster than a living room unit used at weekends, so it is worth doing them all on the same cycle rather than waiting for the worst one to drip.

If you are moving out, service the aircon before the handover inspection and keep the invoice. It is one of the first things a landlord or agent asks for.

Common questions

How much is aircon servicing in Singapore?

On our 2026 jobs, general servicing has been $25 to $30 per unit when all the units are done in one visit. A chemical wash is $110 per unit and a gas top-up is $120. A chemical overhaul is quoted after we open the unit, because it depends on how choked the coil and blower are.

How often should I service my aircon?

Every three months for units that run daily, which is also what most Singapore tenancy agreements ask tenants to do. Units used only at night can stretch to every four to six months. If cooling drops or the unit starts to drip or smell, do not wait for the next cycle.

What is the difference between a chemical wash and a chemical overhaul?

A chemical wash cleans the coil and blower with chemical while the fan coil stays on the wall. A chemical overhaul takes the fan coil down and strips it so the blower wheel, coil, tray and casing are cleaned separately. An overhaul is for badly choked or long-neglected units; most homes only ever need a wash.

My aircon is leaking water. Do I need a gas top-up?

Usually not. Water dripping from the indoor unit is almost always a choked drain pipe or tray, which a general service clears. Gas only comes into it when cooling is weak and the pressure reads low, and if gas keeps running low there is a leak in the piping that needs repairing rather than refilling.
