Office furniture installation: what it costs per piece

Hossain Md Iqbal · Project Lead, RK E&C · Updated 5 November 2026 · 8 min read

Office furniture installation is priced per piece, by size and complexity, not by the hour. On a recent RK E&C quote, installing 121 pieces across two floors came to $17,750, labour only, with the client supplying the furniture. Two lines in that quote had nothing to do with assembly: carrying the second floor's items up by staircase, because there was no lift, and disposing of the packaging. Those are the costs that catch office moves out.

Every rate below is from that quote. Each covers unpacking, assembly and placing the piece where it goes.

Big tables cost more because they are heavy, arrive in several boxes, and need levelling across multiple legs or bases. Workstations with screen panels and side storage take the longest of the everyday items.

The extras that move the total

  • No lift. In that quote, Level 2 had no lift, so all 54 of its items were carried up the stairs at $20 per item, $1,080 in total. Ask the building about lift access, and any rules on using it, before you book.
  • Packaging disposal. Office furniture arrives in a lot of cardboard and foam. The quote included four disposal trips at $350 each, because the furniture was delivered in batches. Fewer, larger deliveries can mean fewer trips.
  • Batched deliveries. If the supplier delivers in stages, the installers either wait or come back. Line up the delivery schedule with the installation schedule.
  • Building rules. Commercial buildings often require works to be booked with management, sometimes outside office hours, with protection for lifts and floors.

Planning the installation

  • A floor plan with every piece marked saves time on the day, especially for rows of workstations.
  • Power and data need to be where the desks will be. If points need adding, sort that before the furniture goes in; see electrical works.
  • A full list from the supplier, with sizes, lets the installer quote per piece accurately, the way the quote above was built.
  • Room to unpack. A clear area for boxes keeps the job moving.

Frequently asked questions

How much does office furniture installation cost in Singapore?

It is priced per piece. On a recent RK E&C quote, office chairs were $50 each, a straight workstation with screen panels $200, an L-shape workstation with screens and side storage $300, and a 4.8-metre boardroom table $700. The whole job, 121 pieces over two floors, came to $17,750, labour only.

Do installers charge extra if there is no lift?

Usually. In that quote, the 54 items for a floor with no lift were carried up by staircase at $20 per item, $1,080 in total.

Is packaging disposal included in office furniture installation?

Not always, so ask. The quote above included four disposal trips at $350 each, because the furniture arrived in batches.

How is office furniture installation priced?

Per piece, by size and complexity, covering unpacking, assembly and placement. It is not normally priced by the hour.

Can you install office furniture we bought ourselves?

Yes. The quote above was labour only, with all furniture supplied by the client. A full list with sizes lets us quote per piece.
